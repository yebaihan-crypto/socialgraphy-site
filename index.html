没有第三方服务器。数据只存在你的设备与私有 iCloud，开发者也无法读取。No third-party servers. Data lives only on your device and private iCloud — even we cannot read it.

互动越多，行星越亮、轨道越靠近中心。它随你的记录慢慢养成。The more you log, the brighter the planet and closer its orbit. It grows with you.
